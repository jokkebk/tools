Match the reference's soft pastel storybook painted rendering, gentle light, full-body front-facing portrait on a softly painted pale lavender/cream background with a few small pumpkins. Cute and comforting, suitable for a two year old at bedtime, no scary elements, no fangs, no lettering, no text, no watermark. Save the generated image so it can be used as a project asset.
